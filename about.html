Our Story

Started as a small counter, grown on trust

Shiddivinayak Electricals began in 2010 as a small counter selling wires and switches. Word of mouth from happy customers — and a habit of fixing things honestly and fairly — grew it into the full-service electrical shop it is today.

We now stock everything from house wiring to smart home devices, and repair everything from tube lights to television sets, for homes, shops and offices across the area.
